Wobblescope — spin, shake, storm & syzygy

Wobblescope is a time-synchronized Earth-system explorer: one scrubber across polar motion, LOD, geomagnetic indices, ocean SST, earthquakes, volcanoes, space weather, and more.

It was inspired by ECDO-adjacent pole visualizations, but its public default is a LIVE measured lobby — not a dual-state prophecy UI.

Use the instrument to

  • Inspect IERS polar motion (meters-scale) without calling it 104° TPW
  • Watch magnetic poles and field-line pedagogy next to Kp/Dst
  • Compare ocean SST / ENSO with other lanes on one clock
  • Keep epistemic badges visible (measured · modeled · exploratory)

Use this site to

  • Learn State 1 / State 2 language
  • Read claim vs measurement on Signals
  • Follow sources and interview provenance
  • Keep hypothesis quarantined from the LIVE lobby
