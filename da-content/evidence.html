Evidence touchpoints

Exploratory touchpoints cited in ECDO presentations — not a closed proof set.

Inundation, not “flood”

ECDO prefers inundation / oceanic displacement: basins leave their places when the outer body reorients quickly, rather than rivers rising from rain alone. Oral histories (Gilgamesh, Atrahasis, global flood corpora) are treated as compressed reports of that class of event — an interpretive choice, not a settled archaeological method.

Monuments and Np′

A large program of work claims many pre- and post-catastrophe monuments align to Np′ (State 2 geographic north) rather than modern true north — including sites repeatedly named in talks: Giza, Nazca, Göbekli Tepe Pillar 43 interpretations, Andean complexes, and others.

How to hold this: as a falsifiable geometric claim requiring open data, error budgets, and independent re-measurement — not as a finished verdict.

Pyramid dual geometry

Seager’s summary of the theory highlights dual orientation features (e.g. shafts) said to register both Polaris positions ~104° apart. Treat as a specific architectural claim tied to Giza literature in ECDO articles; verify against primary surveys before elevating beyond exploratory.

Chronology sketch (theory-side)

Talks often place major ECDO-related inundation and recovery language around multi-millennial bands (including discussions near ~7,000 years before present and other markers). Exact dating is contested even within the hypothesis space; this site does not publish a countdown.

What would raise confidence

  • Public, reproducible monument azimuth datasets with uncertainties
  • Clear separation of cultural memory vs instrumental paleomagnetism
  • Independent core–mantle heat and mass models that survive differential revision (see TES On Modeling ECDO Theory)
  • Falsification paths that can kill the dual-state picture without rhetoric
