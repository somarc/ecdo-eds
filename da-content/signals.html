Signals

What ECDO treats as system indicators — and how to keep claim separate from measurement.

Style
highlight

Rule: a measured time series can be real while the ECDO interpretation remains exploratory. The instrument shows the former; this page labels the latter.

Geomagnetic weakening

Claim (ECDO): falling magnetic moment “permission” unlocks outer-body reorientation.

Measurable: dipole moment reconstructions, IGRF/WMM epochs, observatory networks, Kp/Dst/AE storm indices.

Class: indices = measured/modeled · catastrophe link = exploratory

Magnetic north pole motion

Claim (ECDO): accelerated dip-pole migration can be a precursor signature.

Measurable: secular variation of dip poles from field models (km/yr scale).

Class: pole path = modeled · ECDO meaning = exploratory

Ocean-first heat

Claim (ECDO): exothermic core/mantle processes heat abyssal oceans before atmosphere; 2023-style ocean-first spikes are fingerprints.

Measurable: ocean heat content, ARGO, SST, ENSO indices — with standard climate attribution debates intact.

Class: OHC/SST = measured · core-driven narrative = exploratory

Polar motion & LOD

Claim risk: conflating IERS polar motion (meters) with 104° TPW.

Measurable: IERS EOP C04, LOD, atmospheric angular momentum coupling (established physics).

Class: EOP/LOD/AAM = measured · ECDO TPW = exploratory

How to read a “signal” here

  1. Name the observable and its catalog (e.g. IERS, NOAA, NASA).
  2. State the ECDO interpretation in one sentence.
  3. Mark the interpretation exploratory unless it is standard geophysics.
  4. Prefer co-display of independent lanes over single-metric alarm.

That discipline is why the sister project is a multi-lane instrument, not a single “ECDO meter.”
