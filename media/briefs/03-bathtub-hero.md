# Brief 03 — Bathtub Earth video hero

**Use:** Homepage `video-hero` background  
**Deliverable:** `media/hero-bathtub-ecdo.mp4` (~10s loop-friendly)

## Story beats (keyframed)

1. **Calm** — North America facing, exaggerated topography, State 1 basin  
2. **Slosh start** — shell begins TPW reorientation; shelves overtop  
3. **Inundation** — bathtub-earth water sheets over lowlands  
4. **Settle** — waters recede into basins/deserts; new orientation  

## Constraints

- No text, logos, UI, people  
- Abyssal Instrument palette (cyan / void / copper rim)  
- Prefer continuous crossfade assembly when native video gen is unavailable (ZDR)
